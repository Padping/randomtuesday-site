Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This policy explains what information Random Tuesday, a trading name of [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us"), collects and what we do with it. It covers this website and the apps we publish, including PadPing.

The short version. PadPing keeps your home's information on your own device. We do not have accounts, we do not run a server that stores your data, and we cannot see what you enter into the app. This website collects only the standard technical logs needed to serve pages, plus whatever you choose to put in an email to us.

Information PadPing collects

PadPing works entirely on your device. The information you enter — your home profile, the tasks you track, completion history, equipment install dates and any notes — is stored locally on that device and is not transmitted to us.

Home profile and ZIP code Your device only You

PadPing does not require an account, does not ask you to sign in, and does not include a feature that uploads your data to us. If you delete the app, the data it stored goes with it — so if that data matters to you, keep your own record of it as well.

Children

PadPing is intended for adults managing a home. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Information this website collects

This site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like any web host, Netlify automatically records technical information when a page is requested — including your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs exist to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse. We do not use them to build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share them.

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party advertising or social tracking scripts.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it to answer you, and we keep the correspondence only as long as it is useful for support purposes. If you email us to ask about a beta, we use your address to tell you about that beta and for nothing else.

What we never do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your information to train machine-learning models.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to operate: Netlify for website hosting, Google Workspace for email, and the Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution. Each processes information under its own terms, and Apple and Google may collect information about app downloads and purchases independently of us. Their privacy policies govern that activity.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because PadPing stores your data on your own device, in most cases we simply do not hold anything to give you — but if you believe we do, write to privacy@randomtuesday.app and we will respond within 30 days.

California residents may exercise rights under the CCPA/CPRA at the same address. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by that law.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes affecting an app will also be noted in that app's release notes.
