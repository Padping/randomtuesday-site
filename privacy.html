Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This policy explains what information Random Tuesday, a trading name of RANDOM TUESDAY LLC ("we", "us"), collects through this website and when you write to us, and what we do with it.

The short version. This website collects only the standard technical logs needed to serve pages, plus whatever you choose to put in an email to us. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, which says exactly what that app stores and sends.

Our apps

PadPing. PadPing's privacy policy is at padping.co/privacy. In short: your tasks, home details and notes stay on your device, and PadPing sends only anonymous usage data, plus the text of a template suggestion if you choose to send one. That page is the one we keep current for the app, and the one the app stores link to.

Information this website collects

This site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like any web host, Netlify automatically records technical information when a page is requested, including your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs exist to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse. We do not use them to build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share them.

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party advertising or social tracking scripts.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it to answer you, and we keep the correspondence only as long as it is useful for support purposes. If you email us to ask about a beta, we use your address to tell you when that beta opens and when the app launches, and for nothing else.

What we never do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your information to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not record your screen.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to operate: Netlify for website hosting, PostHog for anonymous product analytics, Google Workspace for email, and the Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution. Each processes information under its own terms, and Apple and Google may collect information about app downloads and purchases independently of us. Their privacy policies govern that activity.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. We hold very little: the correspondence you send us, email addresses on an app's early access list, and for our apps, the anonymous data their own policies describe. To make a request, write to privacy@randomtuesday.app and we will respond within 30 days.

California residents may exercise rights under the CCPA/CPRA at the same address. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by that law.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top updates. Changes to what an app collects are made on that app's own privacy policy.
