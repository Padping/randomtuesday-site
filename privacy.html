Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Your tasks, your home details and your notes never leave your device.

This policy explains what information Random Tuesday, a trading name of RANDOM TUESDAY LLC ("we", "us"), collects and what we do with it. It covers this website and the apps we publish, including PadPing.

The short version. PadPing keeps your home's information, tasks and notes on your own device — there are no accounts and no server holding them. We also collect a small amount of anonymous usage data, like whether setup was completed, so we can tell whether the app works; it never includes anything you typed. This website collects only the standard technical logs needed to serve pages, plus whatever you choose to put in an email to us.

What PadPing stores

PadPing keeps your home details, your task list and your notes on your device. There are no accounts, no login and no server holding your information. We cannot see your tasks, because they are never sent to us.

WhatWhere it livesWho can see it
Home profile and ZIP code Your device only You
Tasks, due dates, completion history Your device only You
Equipment and install dates Your device only You
Reminder schedules Your device only, via the operating system's local notifications You

If you delete the app, that data goes with it. There is no backup on our side to restore from.

What we measure

We collect a small amount of anonymous usage data so we can tell whether the app actually works — whether people finish setup, whether reminders get acted on, whether anyone comes back after a week. Specifically:

  • whether setup was completed or skipped, and how many tasks were matched
  • when a first task gets marked done, and how long after setup
  • whether the app is opened again a week later
  • when a custom task is created — its category, season and frequency only

None of this includes anything you typed. We never receive task names, notes, your address, or any detail about your home. There is no account to attach it to, and we do not try to identify you. This is handled by PostHog, our analytics provider.

We do not record your screen, we do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not sell or share this data with anyone.

Suggesting a template

If you create a task PadPing didn't already know about, you can choose to send it to us as a template suggestion. When you tap that, a box opens pre-filled with the task name — you can edit it to anything you like before sending. We receive the text in that box and the category, and nothing else.

This only happens when you tap send. If you never use it, nothing is ever sent.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone, not by us. Nothing is transmitted for a reminder to fire, and no notification content passes through our systems.

Children

PadPing is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Information this website collects

This site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like any web host, Netlify automatically records technical information when a page is requested — including your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs exist to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse. We do not use them to build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share them.

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party advertising or social tracking scripts.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it to answer you, and we keep the correspondence only as long as it is useful for support purposes. If you email us to ask about a beta, we use your address to tell you about that beta and for nothing else.

What we never do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your information to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not record your screen.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to operate: Netlify for website hosting, PostHog for anonymous product analytics, Google Workspace for email, and the Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution. Each processes information under its own terms, and Apple and Google may collect information about app downloads and purchases independently of us. Their privacy policies govern that activity.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because PadPing stores your data on your own device, in most cases we simply do not hold anything to give you — but if you believe we do, write to privacy@randomtuesday.app and we will respond within 30 days.

California residents may exercise rights under the CCPA/CPRA at the same address. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by that law.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top updates. Material changes affecting an app will also be noted in that app's release notes.
