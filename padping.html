PadPing

Owning a home means a hundred small jobs nobody ever taught you about, and a handful of expensive ones you only learn about too late. PadPing keeps track of both — and only ever shows you the next few.

What it does

Shows what's due next

A short list — never a wall of tasks. Marking something done takes one tap from the home screen, and the next date is worked out for you.

Learns your house, briefly

A few optional questions about your home set up a starting plan. Skip them and you still get the safety-critical basics, never guesses about equipment you may not own.

Only the jobs that matter

Not a chore list. Every task in PadPing is one where skipping it costs real money later — and the app tells you what that cost looks like.

Tracks what wears out

Roof, water heater, HVAC, major appliances. Typical lifespans and your own install dates, kept on their own screen so they never clutter the day-to-day list.

Reminds you, quietly

Scheduled reminders on your device at sensible moments. No streaks, no scores, no red badges counting how far behind you are.

Stays on your phone

No account, no sign-in, no server. Your home's details are stored on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Status

PadPing is in active development and has not been released yet. It is planned for release on the App Store and Google Play in 2026, with a limited beta before then.

If you'd like to be told when the beta opens, email hello@randomtuesday.app with "PadPing beta" in the subject line. We'll use your address for that one message and nothing else.
